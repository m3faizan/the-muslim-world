About Al Fuqayr Well

Al Fuqayr Well is one of Madinah’s most significant heritage sites, deeply connected to the Prophet (peace be upon him) and the story of the Companion Salman al-Farisi "may Allah be pleased with him".
Located beside Salman al-Farisi’s mosque and farm, the well draws from underground water linked to Wadi Bathan, standing as a living symbol of faith, work, and divine blessing.

You don't need tickets to enter.

Location

The well lies in the Al-Shuraybat district of Madinah, near Salman al-Farisi’s mosque and farm.
It is approximately 4 kilometers southwest of the Prophet’s Mosque, making it an easily reachable stop for visitors to the holy city.

The Story Behind the Name

The well takes its name from the nearby farm tied to Salman al-Farisi’s story of freedom.
His master agreed to grant him liberty in exchange for 300 fruitful palm trees. The Prophet (peace be upon him) invited his companions to help plant them, leaving one tree unplanted.
The Prophet (peace be upon him) then said, “Go and dig for it,” and planted it himself.
By Allah’s will, that palm took root and flourished within the same year, a moment of faith and miracle that gave the well its lasting blessing.

Historical Significance

The land surrounding Al Fuqayr Well once belonged to a Companion martyred in the Battle of Uhud, who had dedicated his wealth as charity.
The Prophet (peace be upon him) used part of this land, including the well and its farm, as one of Islam’s earliest charitable endowments (waqf).
Today, the Government of Saudi preserves and restores the site as part of its mission to safeguard Madinah’s prophetic landmarks.
